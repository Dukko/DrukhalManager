# Launching and Saves

**Launch** starts Stellaris with your load order, saving it first if you have unsaved changes. If your last save was played with different mods, it asks before going on. In the Flatpak, the game starts through Steam.

The arrow next to Launch holds the other ways in.

## Continue

**Continue** loads your last save straight away, skipping the menus. It shows the empire and date it will load.

## Saves…

Lists the campaigns you've played from DrukhalManager, with the collection and mods each was played with. It tells you when a campaign's mods have changed since, which is a common reason saves break.

## Multiplayer Check…

Compares your game and mods with other players'. See [[Multiplayer Check]].

## DLC…

Turns DLC on or off for your next game.

## Find the Crashing Mod…

Finds which mod crashes the game by testing halves of your load order. See [[Finding the Crashing Mod]].
