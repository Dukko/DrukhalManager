# Finding the Crashing Mod

When the game crashes and you don't know why, DrukhalManager can find the mod responsible by turning off part of your mods at a time.

## Before you start

Use a new game for each test, or a save you don't mind losing, and **don't save** during the tests. A save loaded with mods turned off can break.

## Steps

1. Open the **Launch** dropdown and choose **Find the Crashing Mod…**. It says roughly how many launches it will take. Press **Start**.
2. A banner at the top says which mods are on for this test. Launch the game, do what made it crash, then quit.
3. When the game closes, DrukhalManager asks **Did Stellaris Crash?** If the game left a crash report, it says so and picks **Yes** for you. Answer **Yes** if it crashed, froze or showed the problem, otherwise **No**.
4. It halves the suspects and asks you to launch again, until one mod is left.

Mods that other mods need stay on together with them. When it's done, or when you choose **Stop Looking**, your collection and load order come back as they were.

To stop partway, choose **Find the Crashing Mod…** again from the Launch dropdown.
