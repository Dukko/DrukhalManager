# Collections and Freezing

A **collection** is a named load order. Each one is synced to its own Paradox Launcher playset, and each has its own merged patch (see [[Conflicts and AI Merging]]).

## Switching and making collections

Click the collection name at the top left. From there you can switch collections, make a new one, and import:

* a Paradox Launcher playset,
* a Workshop collection link,
* a load order someone pasted.

**Copy Load Order** gives you a numbered list with Workshop links, to paste to a friend.

## Freezing a collection

Steam updates mods whenever their authors publish, which can break a campaign in the middle. **Freeze This Collection…**, in the collection menu, copies its Workshop mods into local mods and switches the collection to the copies.

* Fixes the authors publish are missed too, until you unfreeze.
* The health strip tells you when Steam has newer versions of frozen mods.
* **Unfreeze** switches back to the Workshop mods and deletes the copies.
